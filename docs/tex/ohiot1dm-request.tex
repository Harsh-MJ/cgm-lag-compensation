\textbf{How access works (checked 26 Nov 2026 on the dataset pages at
webpages.charlotte.edu/rbunescu):} one page says to email Razvan Bunescu
(\href{mailto:razvan.bunescu@charlotte.edu}{\nolinkurl{razvan.bunescu@charlotte.edu}})
with the subject line \texttt{OhioT1DM\ Request} and give full name, job
title and institutional email; you then get a link to an encrypted
archive and a password (allow about one week). Another page says to
complete the DUA form and email the signed form to the technology
coordinator at Ohio University (Ms. Li Guo). Both pages exist, so send
the request to Bunescu with the DUA attached and ask which route
applies. The dataset is not redistributed here, and it must not be
downloaded from third-party copies.

\textbf{Who should apply:} a DUA is normally signed by a faculty member
or supervisor. Ask your DH 803 instructor whether they sign as the
researcher, with you and Shreyas listed as team members.
