cross-check (30 Oct
2026)}\label{piece-4-kalman-filter-rts-smoother-and-regularised-inversion-cross-check-30-oct-2026}}

Code: \texttt{code/kalman.py}, \texttt{code/test\_kalman.py},
\texttt{code/run\_kalman.py}, \texttt{code/plot\_kalman.py};
\texttt{code/harness.py} was updated (batched estimators). Written by
Claude (AI assistant); the team must read and sign off before claiming
it. Setup as in Piece 3 (27 subjects, 5-fold subject-wise CV, 5-min
sampling, 5\% dropouts); noise sd 2 and 8 mg/dL, scenarios S0 to S5.

\hypertarget{method}{%
\subsection{Method}\label{method}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Model: dG\_I/dt = (G\_B - G\_I)/tau, dG\_B/dt = R, dR/dt = sqrt(q)
  white noise, y = G\_I + noise. Exact discretisation (F and Q checked
  against Van Loan). Only qe = q / sigma\^{}2 matters; tuned on a
  half-decade grid 1e-7 to 1.
\item
  M1f: causal Kalman filter (real time). M1s: RTS smoother (uses
  future). M2: regularised inversion solved as a sparse KKT system
  (independent implementation, second-difference penalty). Variants: tau
  fixed at 10 min ("fixed"), told the true tau ("oracle"), population
  tau tuned jointly with qe on training subjects ("taupop"; needs
  reference glucose, so simulation only).
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{tests-10-of-10-pass}{%
\subsection{Tests: 10 of 10 pass}\label{tests-10-of-10-pass}}

F and Q match matrix-exponential results (1e-16); noise-free ramp with
correct tau recovered to 0.017 mg/dL (filter and smoother); wrong tau (8
vs 10) gives -4.02 mg/dL, theory -4; filter is causal, smoother is not;
batch equals single; no NaNs with dropouts; smoother and regularised
inversion agree to 0.05 to 0.09 mg/dL against about 10 mg/dL error.

\hypertarget{findings-paired-differences-are-median-over-subjects-95-bootstrap-ci}{%
\subsection{Findings (paired differences are median over subjects, 95\%
bootstrap
CI)}\label{findings-paired-differences-are-median-over-subjects-95-bootstrap-ci}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Implementation is validated: the independent regularised inversion
  matches the smoother (M2 minus M1s: 0.0 {[}-0.0, 0.1{]} at S0, noise
  8).
\item
  Real-time filtering is noise-limited. At noise sd 2 (S0) the Kalman
  filter beats naive inversion by 2.1 {[}0.9, 2.6{]} mg/dL and reaches
  the non-causal shift (7.5 vs 8.0), with zero residual lag. At noise sd
  8 it does not beat naive inversion (0.0 {[}-0.5, 3.0{]}) and recovers
  only about 3 mg/dL of the 10 mg/dL lag error versus raw.
\item
  Retrospective smoothing beats the non-causal shift when the model is
  right: S0 noise 8, M1s - B1 = -1.9 {[}-3.3, -1.3{]} mg/dL (p
  \textless{} 0.001); and cuts false alarms to 0.23 per day versus 0.93
  raw with the same detection (57 of 58 events).
\item
  Knowing tau does not help the real-time filter: oracle minus fixed =
  -0.1 {[}-0.1, 0.1{]} at S0 noise 8 and -0.2 {[}-0.9, 0.0{]} at noise
  2. Noise, not tau error, is the bottleneck.
\item
  Unmodelled sensor lag matters for the smoother: in S5 noise 8
  fixed-tau smoother is worse than the shift by 3.3 {[}1.6, 4.1{]}
  mg/dL, and tuning a population tau recovers it (M1s tuned minus fixed
  -3.8 {[}-5.3, -1.8{]}; tuned tau 16 to 18 min).
\item
  A tuned tau is not the physical lag: at S0 noise 8 the real-time
  filter\textquotesingle s tuned tau is 6 min (below the true mean
  11.9), acting as noise regularisation.
\item
  Real-time filtering raises false alarms: S0 noise 8 hypoglycaemia
  false alarms 1.24 per day versus 0.93 raw, with earlier alarms (-11.9
  min versus -4.9); do not read earlier alerts as better without the
  false-alarm cost.
\item
  Grid edges: no Kalman or regularised-inversion picks at a grid edge
  after widening the regularisation grid; only B3 at noise 2 picks the
  smallest window (3 samples).
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{caveats}{%
\subsection{Caveats}\label{caveats}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Tuned-tau and shift results use ground-truth glucose to tune; real
  data only has sparse fingersticks (addressed in Piece 5).
\item
  Filter treats noise as white; simulated noise has a slow component. An
  AR(1) noise state is a candidate ablation.
\item
  Posterior coverage of the filter\textquotesingle s intervals is not
  yet checked.
\end{itemize}
