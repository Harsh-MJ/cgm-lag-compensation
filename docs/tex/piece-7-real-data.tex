\hypertarget{what-the-check-does}{%
\subsection{What the check does}\label{what-the-check-does}}

Parser: CGM (5 min), fingersticks, meals, boluses, basal and temp basal
to a 5-min grid and the Piece 6 input drives. For each left-out patient,
tau, process noise and sensor noise are chosen to minimise fingerstick
error on the other patients; the held-out patient is scored on its own
fingersticks: raw CGM, smoother with tau = 10, tuned smoother, with and
without the input prior. Tests (synthetic): loader reads CGM,
fingersticks, meals and boluses; the grid has 5-min samples and about
5\% gaps; the drives are non-negative; the leave-one-patient-out run
completes and the smoother beats raw CGM against the synthetic
fingersticks (8.1 vs 7.9 mg/dL, a plumbing check only).

\hypertarget{cautions-for-the-real-run}{%
\subsection{Cautions for the real run}\label{cautions-for-the-real-run}}

Fingersticks are noisy (about 5 to 10\%), sparse, usually taken before
meals, and on some sensors were used to calibrate the CGM, so agreement
with them is not the same as accuracy against blood glucose. Report this
as exploratory. Per-patient lag will be as unidentifiable on real data
as in simulation; the useful output is the population lag chosen on
other patients.
